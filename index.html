AUTOMATION PLATFORMSJ DESIGN STUDIO × SAMSUNG

SJ Design Automation Hub

Built a local web application that turns a fragmented Samsung publishing process—spanning Excel, Jira, AEM, and country sites—into one guided workflow using the user’s existing SSO session. It processes pending locales from a workbook, finds the matching Jira ticket by locale and URL slug, advances required workflow stages, publishes pages, verifies the public URL, and writes the confirmed result back to the workbook.

Separate automations audit localized FAQ pages against a baseline while keeping editorial approval human-led, and copy approved master AEM components to child sites with duplicate protection and targeted retries. One dashboard manages sign-in, workbook selection, per-country progress, live status, errors, and raw logs.

≈80% less workflow time · dozens of hours saved monthly
